What PJ is

A journal built around the easiest way to capture a thought — your voice — without giving up a shred of privacy.

🎙️

Talk or type

Say whatever's on your mind and PJ writes it down, or type it yourself. From your wrist, your phone, or your Mac — whatever's closest.

🔒

Private & encrypted

Every entry is end-to-end encrypted. It's yours alone — not even we can read it. Your words never become anyone else's business.

☁️

On all your devices

Write on your iPhone, read on your Mac. Everything syncs privately across your own Apple devices through your iCloud.

🔎

Yours to keep

Find any entry, bring in an old journal, and copy, print, or export your words whenever you want. Your journal, your call.

The privacy promise

PJ is end-to-end encrypted: your entries are stored as unreadable ciphertext, and the key lives only on your devices. We can't read your journal, we don't sell anything, and there are no ads. A journal should be the one place you can be completely honest.
