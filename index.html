Why PJ exists

A journal only works if it's safe. I learned that the hard way: there was a stretch of my life when the things I wrote for myself didn't stay mine. So the first rule for PJ was simple — your thoughts belong to you. The second came from the late nights. Sometimes you don't need advice. You need five minutes to say it out loud and put it somewhere.

— Jack, who built PJ

How people use PJ

It started as a place for the hard nights. It turned into more than that.

🌙

The five-minute reset

On call all week, up all night, then a hard day on top of it. Five minutes of talking into PJ, got it out of her system, then a nap.

🩺

A caregiver's notebook

Keeping track of a loved one's health — the good days, the bad days, what changed, what the doctor said — so it's all there at the next appointment.

🎖️

Stories in their own voice

Sitting down with a parent to record the stories from a long career, while the memories are still clear, so the family can hear them for years to come.

🏡

Getting through a change

A separation, a move, a quieter house. Somewhere to put it at night, without anyone reading over your shoulder.

No one goes without PJ because of money

PJ was built to help people, not to make money. If the cost is truly what stands in your way, write to support@pjjournal.com and tell us a little about your situation. We read every request personally and set aside a number of free copies for people who need them.

Counselors, therapists and support groups: write to us. We'd love to talk about how PJ could help the people you work with.

The privacy promise

Your words, labels, recordings and photos are end-to-end encrypted: stored as unreadable ciphertext, with a key that lives only on your devices. We can't read your journal, we don't sell anything, and there are no ads. A journal should be the one place you can be completely honest.
