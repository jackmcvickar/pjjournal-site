How to record someone

No setup, no account. It works at the kitchen table.

1. Ask one question

Open PJ, tap Start talking, and hand them the phone. One good question is enough to get going.

2. Let them talk

Don't interrupt and don't worry about typing. PJ writes the words down for you, right on your device.

3. Keep their voice

The recording stays with the entry. Tap Listen to this any time to hear it again.

4. Keep their stories together

Add a label with their name, and use Follow Up when they remember more later, so one story can continue where it left off.

5. Start from a photo

Pick an old photo and ask, "Tell me about this one." Photos bring back details nobody remembers on the spot.

6. Ask first

Tell them you're recording, and why. Let them hear it back. Most people love being asked.

Questions that get people talking

Pick one. Save the rest for next time.

What's your earliest memory?

What was the house you grew up in like? Walk me through it.

How did you meet Mom (or Dad, or your best friend)?

What was your first job, and what did it pay?

Tell me about a holiday you'll never forget.

Who taught you the most, and what did they teach you?

What were you doing when you were my age?

What's something you want the grandkids to know?

If you served: where were you stationed, and who were your friends there?

What are you proudest of?

For caregivers

When you're looking after someone, a lot happens between appointments.

Keep track of the days

Talk for a minute at night about how today went: the good, the hard, what changed. Label it with their name so it's all in one place.

Bring it to the appointment

Print an entry or save it as a PDF to bring along, so nothing gets lost between visits.

PJ is a journal, not a medical record. It doesn't connect to doctors, health systems or anyone else; what you share is up to you.

Private by design

Their words, recordings and photos are end-to-end encrypted, with a key only your devices hold. No account, no ads, nothing collected. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.
