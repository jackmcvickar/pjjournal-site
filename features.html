Everything PJ does

A private journal you can just talk to, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. One purchase, no subscription, no ads.

Capture

Just talk. Or type. Whatever's closest.

Talk or type

Say what's on your mind and PJ writes it down, or type it yourself. Your voice is turned into text right on your device, never sent anywhere to be transcribed.

Keep your voice, not just the words

Your recording stays with the entry, so you can tap Listen to this and hear it again. On the Mac you can choose to keep only the words.

From your wrist

One button on your Apple Watch, hands-free, in the car or on a walk. Check my Watch shows what's on its way to your phone.

Keep it safe

Private by design, with ways back if something goes wrong.

Encrypted, in your own iCloud

Your words, labels, recordings and photos are end-to-end encrypted on your device and synced only through your own iCloud. No accounts, no server, no ads, nothing collected.
